## About

I work at the intersection of finance and data analysis, with a focus on portfolio construction, valuation, and investment research — increasingly applied to the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) and other frontier markets.

I hold a BBA from Pokhara University's School of Environmental Science and Management and am currently completing an MBA specializing in Finance and Control at SOMTU. I founded the SOMTU Finance Club and lead its Finance Lab Committee, and I work as a licensed insurance agent at Sanima Reliance Life Insurance.

This repository space is where I develop financial models and research tools — mostly in Excel/Google Sheets and Python — rather than general-purpose software.
